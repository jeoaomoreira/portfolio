01 — Direction

What Raíz stands for

The feeling every page, color, and word should create.

Raíz is warm, grounded, and trustworthy — modern clarity and craft, built for the people who run Guanacaste.

Who we design for

A Guanacaste small-business owner — a soda, a hotel, a tour operator — not a design-savvy creative. The brand has to feel inviting and credible to them on the first glance.

We are

Warm & rooted

Earthy color, a little editorial craft, and plain language that builds trust. Local at heart, modern in execution.

We are not

The "creative studio" cliché

We explicitly rejected dark, monochrome, animation-heavy design — it reads as cold and intimidating to our audience.

02 — Logo

The mark & wordmark

A sprout breaking from its roots — growth grounded in place. The wordmark is set in Fraunces.

Raíz Primary lockup · on canvas
Raíz Reversed · on brand green
Mark only · on surface white
Raíz On ink · single-color dark
Do

Give it room

Keep clear space of at least the height of the mark on every side. The mark is a single path and inherits currentColor — recolor via CSS, never repaint by hand.

Don't

Distort or recolor off-palette

No stretching, rotating, drop shadows, gradients, or colors outside the brand palette. Keep contrast high enough to read at small sizes.

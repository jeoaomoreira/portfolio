01 — Name

Why Nambuku

Where the name comes from — and the one claim we never make.

Nambuku is coined in the sound-world of Chorotega (Mangue), the language of Guanacaste's first people, and built on nambu — the attested element for water.

What is actually on the record

Water appears as nimbu in Brinton's 1886 vocabulary, as nambu in that same source's nambu sangui, and as nimbo inside the real Guanacaste place name Nimboyores. Brinton also records that in Mangue every syllable ends in a vowel — na-mbu-ku does. The whole word, though, is ours: it is not attested anywhere.

Say

Named from nambu, water

“Nambuku takes its name from nambu, the Chorotega word for water — in the driest province of Costa Rica.” Water is the organising idea: scarce here, and the thing every living thing depends on.

Never say

“Nambuku means …”

Chorotega has no living speakers and a thinly recorded lexicon, so the word carries no documented meaning. An invented etymology is the one claim a journalist, a cultural organisation or a Guaitil artisan could check and disprove — and this brand rests on being genuinely from here.

02 — Direction

What Nambuku stands for

The feeling every page, color, and word should create.

Nambuku is clear, confident, and modern — high-contrast craft, built for the people who run Guanacaste.

Who we design for

A Guanacaste small-business owner — a soda, a hotel, a tour operator — not a design-savvy creative. The brand has to feel inviting and credible to them on the first glance.

We are

Clear & confident

Black, white and one kinetic orange, with plain language that builds trust. Local at heart, unmistakably modern in execution.

We are not

The "creative studio" cliché

We explicitly rejected dark, monochrome, animation-heavy design — it reads as cold and intimidating to our audience.

03 — Logo

The mark & wordmark

A lowercase n with an exit stroke, then a dot — it reads as “n.”, the wordmark's initial rather than a picture of anything. The wordmark is set in Geist.

Nambuku Reversed · on Deep Black
Do

Give it room

Keep clear space of at least the height of the mark on every side. The mark is a stroked path plus the dot, and inherits currentColor — recolor via CSS, never repaint by hand. Its viewBox carries 8 units of padding on each side: crop that away and the round caps land on the edge and render chopped.

Don't

Distort or recolor off-palette

No stretching, rotating, drop shadows, gradients, or colors outside the brand palette. Keep contrast high enough to read at small sizes.

04 — Color

Black, white & one kinetic orange

The page carries no color of its own, so the orange earns all the attention. Surfaces are soft-UI: a light grey canvas with white elements lifted by shadows instead of borders. Every text/UI pair is verified ≥ 4.5:1 (WCAG AA) — except large bold text on orange, held to the 3:1 large-text bar as a deliberate brand choice.

Pure White

Clarity, simplicity, minimalism. It creates the breathing space in the layout — and it is what makes the accent register at all.

Deep Black

Strength, confidence, contrast. Carries the dark surfaces and gives the work a premium, deliberate feel.

Neutral Grey

Balance and stability. A soft ground that raises contrast and pushes the main accents forward.

Kinetic Orange

Energy, motion, action. It grabs attention and drives the click — every primary CTA, and nothing else.

WhatsApp runs in the accent, not WhatsApp green, so the palette stays unified. The --color-whatsapp token still exists purely so that decision can be reversed in one edit.

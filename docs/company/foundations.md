# Raíz — Company Foundations

> **Raíz** · a modern web studio rooted in Guanacaste, Costa Rica.
> _Last updated: 2026-11-07_

## 1. One-liner

**Raíz gives small businesses in Guanacaste a modern digital presence — a website, Google visibility, and ongoing care — so they get found, trusted, and chosen by more customers.**

## 2. Vision (where we're headed)

To become **Guanacaste's most trusted web studio** — the first name a local business thinks of when they want to grow online — proving that world-class, modern web work can come from rural Costa Rica.

## 3. Mission (what we do every day)

We help local businesses look as good online as they are in person: modern design, found on Google, and maintained over time — explained in plain language, built as a long-term partnership.

## 4. Positioning statement

For **small businesses in Guanacaste** that struggle to get found and look credible online, **Raíz** is a **local web studio** that delivers a **complete, modern digital presence (site + Google + ongoing care)**. Unlike freelancers, DIY builders, or faraway agencies, Raíz combines **modern design, ongoing care, and local face-to-face trust**.

**The wedge is _place_, not industry.** We serve any local business, but our brand is unmistakably Guanacasteco. Local roots are the differentiator no remote competitor can copy.

**Brand essence:** _Rooted locally, modern globally._

## 5. Core values

- **Raíces** — we're part of this community; we show up in person.
- **Modern craft** — the design quality of a big-city studio.
- **Claridad** — no jargon; clients always understand what they're paying for.
- **Partnership** — we stay (care plans), we don't build-and-vanish.

## 6. Target customer (ICP)

**Who:** Owner-operated local businesses in Guanacaste — restaurants/sodas, hotels & cabinas, tour operators, salons, clinics, trades, and shops — serving both tourists and locals.

**Their situation:** No website, or an outdated / social-media-only presence.

**What they feel:** Invisible on Google, a little embarrassed by their current page, and watching more "professional-looking" competitors win customers.

**What they want:** To look trustworthy, be easy to find, and get more calls/bookings — without having to understand the tech.

## 7. Offer ladder (productized services)

| # | Product | What it is | Billing |
|---|---------|-----------|---------|
| 1 | **Lanzamiento** | New modern website + Google Business Profile setup | One-time project fee |
| 2 | **Renovación** | Redesign/upgrade of an existing site | One-time project fee |
| 3 | **Plan Raíz** (core recurring) | Hosting, maintenance, content updates, basic SEO, support | **Monthly** |
| 4 | **Crecimiento** (future) | Ongoing SEO / local ads / growth work | Monthly retainer |

**Model:** every build (1 or 2) is sold with a **Plan Raíz** attached. The project fee funds the work; the care plan builds **recurring revenue (MRR)** — the key to a stable studio.

> Pricing: set accessible, value-based tiers for the local market. Define actual CRC/USD numbers before launch (separate pricing doc).

## 8. 12-month goals & milestones

Aligned to the two chosen goals: **(a) land first clients + portfolio** and **(b) build a local brand.**

| Quarter | Focus | Milestones |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| **Q1** | Foundation & proof | Brand identity done · Raíz site live · 3 case studies from existing projects · offer + pricing defined · own Google Business Profile |
| **Q2** | First clients | 3–5 paying clients · collect testimonials/photos · referral ask built into delivery |
| **Q3** | Recurring + reach | 8–10 clients total · 2–3 on Plan Raíz (first MRR) · active locally (FB groups, networking, referrals) |
| **Q4** | Local brand | 12–15 clients · ~5 recurring care plans · referrals become the #1 lead source |

**North-star metric:** number of **recurring Plan Raíz clients (MRR)** — it measures both stability and brand trust.

**Track monthly:** new clients · active care plans (MRR) · published case studies · % leads from referral.

## 9. Why this works (strategy notes)

- **Recurring revenue first.** Project fees pay the bills; care plans build the business. Attaching Plan Raíz to every build is the single most important habit.
- **Local trust is the moat.** A remote agency can out-spend you but can't shake hands at the soda. Lean into being Guanacasteco.
- **Showcase = sales engine.** The website's job is to turn completed work into the next client's confidence. Every project becomes a case study.
- **Start with proof.** You already have completed projects — these become the first 3 case studies and remove the "zero credibility" problem most new studios face.

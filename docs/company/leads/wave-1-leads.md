## Method & caveats

- Revised 2026-11-13 after review: small restaurants and sodas are parked because they likely can't pay for a build plus Plan Nambuku. Patagonia del Mar stays as the exception (2,300+ reviews). The parked leads are on the 'Parked – food' tab / wave-1-parked-food.csv.
- Priority: A = 100+ reviews and no real website · B = tourism/transport that depends on third parties · C = the rest. Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Viator, taxi-platform and free builder pages all count as no real website.
- Activity check: each profile's newest review (sorted by Newest) plus its current hours. Dropped anything with no reviews in roughly the last 6 months.
- Dentists: almost every dentist in Tamarindo, Huacas, Flamingo and Potrero already has a real website (Tamarindo Dentist, Dental Solutions, Pacific Smile, SolSide, Costa Dental, America Dental, Coast Dental, Flamingo Dental, Cabo Velas Health, Guanacaste Dental). Huacas Dental Care is the only one without a site, and it's included through Centro Médico Huacas.
- Transport: most established shuttle companies already have sites (Ecotrans, Tamarindo Shuttle, Trip N Taxi, JM, Playa Grande Shuttle, LAR, Issys, Taxi Airport Liberia). Car rentals are mostly chains, which are skipped. Taxi Flamingo TG216 was dropped because its newest review is 2 years old.
- Supplements: Good Energy, our own client, came up and is excluded. Hardly any other supplement-only stores exist in the area, so the closest fits (Mentha & Limón, Farmacia Bohemia) are included.
- Clothing: Tamarindo boutiques mostly have sites (Papaya Con Leche, Azul Profundo, Club 33, More Surf Shop, Matos). Buena Nena and Cala Tamarindo were skipped: too few reviews, stale, or no phone.
- Outside the area (good fits, but not in our towns): Vet Sharine Alice (Playa Hermosa, 107 reviews, no site), Veterinaria San Francisco de Asís (Sardinal, 102, no site) and Veterinaria Hermanos Alfaro (Santa Cruz, 118, no site, unclaimed).
- Tempate: nothing qualified. Access (test 4) isn't verified; phone numbers come from the public listings and no one has been contacted.
